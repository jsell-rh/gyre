- **Auto-filter at >500 nodes** — `canvas-density.js` (pure policy) applied in three production paths: ExplorerCanvas's layout pipeline (probe-verified above), SvgGraphCanvas's render path (tested), and mirrored by the shell. Boundary semantics pinned by tests: exactly 500 → full, 501 → public-only, "public count ≤1000 with total >1000 stays public-only" matches the spec's "exceeding ~1000 *even after filtering*" wording. `isPublicNode` treats missing visibility as public, `pub_crate`/`internal` as private.

- **d3-force for graph** — sync 300-tick simulation (`layout-engines.js`, from task-170, verified here because task-177's renderer depends on it); hardened test (4 same-type nodes, 2 disjoint edges, 4 distinct x positions) fails under the mutation probe.
