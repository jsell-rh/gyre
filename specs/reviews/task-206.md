### F1 — preview liveness (closed)

`launch_taskless_agent_process` (the preview launch path,
spawn.rs:2958-3010) now issues the session X.509-SVID through the same
`spiffe::issue_session_svid` used by `launch_agent_workload`, with the
same stack-evidence resolution, and injects
`GYRE_SPIFFE_ID`/`GYRE_SVID_PEM`/`GYRE_SVID_KEY`/`GYRE_STACK_HASH` into
the workload environment. Verified end-to-end by source inspection:

- The heartbeat chain is real crypto, not a structural check:
  `PUT /api/v1/agents/:id/heartbeat` → `attest_session` →
  `verify_workload_identity` loads the record the launch persisted,
  validates the chain against the published trust bundle (gyre_spiffe
  x509 / ring ECDSA), checks validity window, SPIFFE-ID session binding,
  and exact stack-fingerprint equality. The described pre-fix failure
  mode was confirmed real: no SVID record → "session was never attested"
  → 403 → `last_heartbeat` never advances → `is_alive` falls back to
  `spawned_at` → the stale detector (60 s window) reaps a still-running
  preview agent.
- Runner side needed no change: agent-runner.mjs already presents
  `GYRE_SVID_PEM`/`GYRE_STACK_HASH` every 15 s; the fix supplies exactly
  those env vars.
- Fail-closed: issuance failure fails the launch; the caller routes it to
  `mark_preview_agent_failed` (process kill, Failed status, token
  revoked, budget slot released, worktree removed).
- The new `preview_agent_heartbeats_keep_long_runs_active` test is hard:
  it captures the exact env the process received (empty pre-fix → the
  `soon()` helper panics), drives the runner's heartbeat through the
  production router with the agent's live JWT (403 pre-fix), ages the
  spawn past the detector window and runs a real `stale_agents::run_once`
  pass (must stay Active), asserts GET status reports `running`, and
  cross-checks with a tampered-SVID negative control (403) proving the
  204 was real verification.
